What's New in Version 4.0.1:

New
• Greifensee – added the boats on Greifensee with 5 stations (Maur, Fällanden, Greifensee, Uster, Mönchaltorf), selectable in the list and on the map

Improvements
• More accurate verified distances – ride distance is now measured from speed over time instead of raw GPS points, removing the small overshoot from GPS scatter
• Verified rides now count as waves on the leaderboard (overall and per station), in "My Stations", and toward your station and lake badges
• New verified badges for riding at 3, 5, and 10 different stations
• Riders without a name now appear as "Anonymous", so ranks always match the list

Fixed
• Fixed your leaderboard rank showing a gap when an unnamed rider sat in between

Thank you for using NextWave! We're continuously working to improve your wakethieving experience.
